About

About / Open-source licenses

yravgSubMeetly runs recognition and translation on your device. Audio, video, and transcripts are not uploaded to our servers. Model weights may download once (user-triggered) from Hugging Face or another source you choose, then stay cached locally. Open-source components used:

  • OpenAI Whisper — MIT License
  • Mistral Voxtral — Apache License 2.0
  • Apple MLX / mlx-whisper / mlx-lm (native helper) — MIT License
  • Qwen2.5 (native translation) — Apache License 2.0
  • Hugging Face transformers.js — Apache License 2.0
  • Microsoft ONNX Runtime Web — MIT License
  • Helsinki-NLP Opus-MT models — CC BY 4.0
  • Silero VAD — MIT License
  • @ricky0123/vad-web — ISC License
