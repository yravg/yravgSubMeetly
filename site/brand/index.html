Who Kasi is

A tarsier from the forests of Sulawesi: ears that turn toward every sound, eyes larger than its brain. Kasi hangs under the pill, hears what you hear and writes it down. Kasi never talks. Its job is the product's job: listen, write, translate, keep the minutes.

Marks

Size ladder

The full head with amber eye rims from 64 px up. Below 64 px use the mark: no rims, larger eyes, no mouth. Below 16 px use the app icon or the word.

Model sheet

Front, three-quarter and back. The 180° head turn is Kasi's signature move and plays when captions are translated.

Expressions

The top edge of the face plate is the brow line: raise it for surprise, drop the inner ends for worry. Ears carry the rest.

Rules

Do

  • One Kasi per screen, attached to the pill or standing alone.
  • Hang under the pill. The pill touches the top of the screen, so Kasi is never above it.
  • Let ears and eyes do the acting; keep the mouth small.
  • Use the mark (no rims) under 64 px and inside the 32 px resting pill.
  • Keep the colours flat: fur, shade, ear, face plate, ink, amber, subtitle yellow.

Don't

  • Give Kasi words, speech bubbles or captions of its own. It listens.
  • Show eyes only in the pill: that reads as another app's notch. Always the head with ears.
  • Point the ears sideways past 40° except in hush and doze; flat ears read as Gizmo.
  • Add gradients, outlines, blush or a cat mouth.
  • Put Kasi on real error states. Errors get plain words: what stopped and what to do.

Motion

Blink120 ms, every 2.4–6 s at random. Never while happy or asleep.
Ears listenEach ear twitches up to ±7° every 0.7–1.7 s while sound plays.
Head turn900 ms: front → ¾ → back → ¾ → front, on each translated line.
Drop inCaptions start: Kasi drops from the pill on a spring (0.7 s), then sways ±2.2° every 3.2 s.
