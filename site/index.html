Meet Kasi. Big ears, bigger eyes.

Kasi is a tarsier, a tiny primate from the forests of Sulawesi with ears that turn toward every sound and eyes larger than its brain. It lives on your pill, hears what you hear and writes it down. Kasi never talks. It listens.

  1. ListeningEars up the moment a call or video starts.
  2. WritingPencil out while captions run.
  3. TranslatingTurns its head from one language to the other.
  4. ThinkingDrafting the minutes after the call.
  5. DoneMinutes ready. One clap.
  6. RestingNothing playing. One eye open.

Everything you hear, in words.

Hover the pill to open it. Move away and it closes. Captions start by themselves when a call or a video plays.

Nothing leaves your computer.
